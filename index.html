What is Brix?

Brix is the percentage of sugar in a liquid. Slush machines require mixes to land in the 11–13% range to freeze cleanly without becoming icy or watery.

Too low? The drink freezes rock-solid. Too high? It won’t freeze at all.

SlushIQ calculates your Brix automatically and helps you adjust sugar, water, and alcohol levels to hit the perfect freeze zone every time.
