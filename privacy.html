SlushIQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

SlushIQ (“we”, “us”, “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you:

  • use the SlushIQ mobile application (iOS or Android)
  • visit our website or support pages
  • participate in our online communities

SlushIQ is designed primarily as an offline frozen-drink calculator and recipe tool. We intentionally collect as little information as possible, and we never sell user data.

By using SlushIQ, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

SlushIQ collects very limited information, and only when needed to operate the app or respond to inquiries.

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Email address (only if you voluntarily contact us)
  • Bug reports or feedback you intentionally submit

SlushIQ does not require an account and does not store personal information on servers.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device model, OS version
  • App version
  • Anonymous performance/crash logs (via Apple/Google)
  • Anonymous diagnostics and crash data (via platform services)

This information is anonymous and cannot identify you.

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Names, addresses, or contact info
  • Payment information
  • Precise location
  • Contacts or photos
  • Recipe entries, ingredient lists, mix logs
  • Any personal identifiers

All SlushIQ calculations run locally on your device.

3. How We Use Information

  • Improve app performance
  • Fix bugs or diagnose crashes
  • Respond to support emails
  • Send optional updates if you opted in

We do not sell or share your data with advertisers or third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 Apple App Store & Google Play Services

Used for app distribution, crash reporting, and anonymous analytics.

4.2 Formspree (Email Submissions)

Used for secure email forwarding if you sign up on our website.

4.3 OpenAI API (Brixley Chat)

Brixley Chat uses OpenAI’s API to interpret ingredient descriptions. Only the text you type is transmitted — never personal identifiers, device IDs, accounts, or payment data.

OpenAI does not use API data to train models.

4.4 Advertising

SlushIQ does not display advertisements, perform ad targeting, or engage in cross-app tracking. Advertising identifiers may be accessed by platform services solely for install attribution and analytics purposes.

5. Data Retention

  • Email submissions: kept only as long as needed
  • Crash logs: retained by Apple/Google (typically 30–90 days)

6. Data Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, but no digital system is completely secure.

7. Children’s Privacy

SlushIQ is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

8. Your Rights

SlushIQ does not maintain user accounts or store personal data generated through normal app usage. Anonymous analytics data cannot be linked to an individual user.

If you contact us by email or submit a form, you may request deletion of that specific correspondence by contacting us.

9. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Changes will be reflected with a new “Last Updated” date.
